# Privacy Policy for QR Password Fill

**Last updated:** February 2026

## Overview

QR Password Fill is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

## Data Collection

**We collect nothing.** Specifically:

- ❌ No personal information
- ❌ No passwords or credentials
- ❌ No browsing history
- ❌ No analytics or telemetry
- ❌ No cookies or tracking

## How the Extension Works

1. The extension accesses your device's camera only when you explicitly click "Scan QR Code"
2. QR codes are decoded locally in your browser
3. Scanned content is held in memory only while the scanner tab is open
4. When you close the tab or scan a new code, the previous data is discarded
5. Copied passwords go to your system clipboard (managed by your operating system, not by us)

## Camera Access

The extension requests camera permission solely to scan QR codes. Camera access is:
- Only activated when you initiate a scan
- Never recorded or transmitted
- Stopped immediately when scanning completes

## Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.
